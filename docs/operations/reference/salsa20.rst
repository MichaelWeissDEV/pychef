What it does
------------

Salsa20 is a stream cipher designed by Daniel J. Bernstein and submitted to the eSTREAM project;
Salsa20/8 and Salsa20/12 are round-reduced variants. It is closely related to the ChaCha stream
cipher. Key: Salsa20 uses a key of 16 or 32 bytes (128 or 256 bits). Nonce: Salsa20 uses a nonce of
8 bytes (64 bits). Counter: Salsa uses a counter of 8 bytes (64 bits). The counter starts at zero at
the start of the keystream, and is incremented at every 64 bytes.
