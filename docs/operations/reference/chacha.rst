What it does
------------

ChaCha is a stream cipher designed by Daniel J. Bernstein. It is a variant of the Salsa stream
cipher. Several parameterizations exist; 'ChaCha' may refer to the original construction, or to the
variant as described in RFC-8439. ChaCha is often used with Poly1305, in the ChaCha20-Poly1305 AEAD
construction. Key: ChaCha uses a key of 16 or 32 bytes (128 or 256 bits). Nonce: ChaCha uses a nonce
of 8 or 12 bytes (64 or 96 bits). Counter: ChaCha uses a counter of 4 or 8 bytes (32 or 64 bits);
together, the nonce and counter must add up to 16 bytes. The counter starts at zero at the start of
the keystream, and is incremented at every 64 bytes.
