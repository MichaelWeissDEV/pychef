What it does
------------

The Fletcher checksum is an algorithm for computing a position-dependent checksum devised by John
Gould Fletcher at Lawrence Livermore Labs in the late 1970s. The objective of the Fletcher checksum
was to provide error-detection properties approaching those of a cyclic redundancy check but with
the lower computational effort associated with summation techniques.
