What it does
------------

RC4 (also known as ARC4) is a widely-used stream cipher designed by Ron Rivest. It is used in
popular protocols such as SSL and WEP. Although remarkable for its simplicity and speed, the
algorithm's history doesn't inspire confidence in its security.
