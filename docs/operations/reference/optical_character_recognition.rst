Optical Character Recognition
=============================

What it does
------------

Optical character recognition or optical character reader (OCR) is the mechanical or electronic
conversion of images of typed, handwritten or printed text into machine-encoded text. Supported
image formats: png, jpg, bmp, pbm.

   scope and known boundaries.

.. warning::

   OCR is intentionally limited compared with a general OCR engine.
